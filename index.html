Privacy Policy

App: Ship Out: Harbor Puzzle (com.shipout.app)
Developer: WaveForge
Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Ship Out: Harbor Puzzle ("the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

The app does not require you to create an account, and we do not ask you for your name, e-mail address, phone number, or any other directly identifying information.

The app uses Google AdMob to show advertisements. AdMob and its partners may automatically collect the following data from your device:

  • Device or other identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address (not precise GPS location)
  • App interactions, such as ad impressions and clicks
  • Diagnostics and crash logs, used to measure ad performance and detect errors

2. How we use this information

  • To show and measure advertisements, which help keep the app free
  • To prevent fraud and abuse and to keep the services secure
  • To analyze app and ad performance and fix errors

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings, scores, and similar data are stored locally on your device. We do not receive this data. If you uninstall the app, locally stored data is removed.

4. In-app purchases

If the app offers in-app purchases, they are processed by Google Play. Payment details (such as card numbers) are handled by Google and are never received or stored by us. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so we can unlock the purchased item.

5. Third-party services

The app uses third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Services and Google Play Billing: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data is shared with these providers only to the extent needed to provide advertising, purchases, and the related security and analytics functions.

6. Your choices

  • Advertising ID: You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Consent: In regions where the law requires it (such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice later where the app offers a privacy settings option.

7. Data retention

We do not maintain user accounts or a user database. Data collected by advertising partners is retained according to their own policies.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area, the UK GDPR, or the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law, KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, to object to its processing, or to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold identifying information about you, most of these requests are best directed to the relevant advertising provider, but you are welcome to contact us and we will help where we can.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the "Last updated" date changed accordingly.
